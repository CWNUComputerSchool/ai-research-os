# CWNU AI Research OS Logo Guidelines

## Concept

The logo combines four signals for `西华师范大学AI赋能科研系统`:

- `C`-shaped orbit: CWNU, continuous research workflow, and AI circulation.
- Open book: academic reading, writing, knowledge base, and NotebookLM-style synthesis.
- Radar arcs and nodes: academic radar tracker, literature discovery, and research intelligence.
- `AI` core: the system is an AI-native research operating layer, not only a document tool.

## Name Lockup

Primary Chinese name:

`西华师范大学AI赋能科研系统`

English abbreviation:

`CWNU AI Research OS`

## Colors

- Deep academic ink: `#17202A`
- University/research teal: `#0F766E`
- AI emerald: `#10B981`
- Intelligence blue: `#2563EB`
- Knowledge gold: `#F59E0B`
- Soft interface background: `#F8FAFC`

## Recommended Usage

- Website header: use `cwnu-ai-logo-full.svg` on light backgrounds.
- Dark mode header: use `cwnu-ai-logo-dark.svg`.
- Favicon/app icon/sidebar compact state: use `cwnu-ai-logo-mark.svg`.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the icon diameter.
- Do not recolor the mark into a single flat color unless the target surface requires monochrome printing.
